# Toei GO — English store listing

## App name

Toei GO

## Short description

An unofficial app for checking routes on Toei Transportation services

## Full description

Toei GO helps you check routes and travel times when using services such as Toei Bus and the Toei Subway.

When you use a supported fare pass or welfare transportation program, the app can display the amount to pay based on the option you selected. The app does not determine whether you are eligible for any program. Always check the official information for current eligibility and usage conditions.

**This is an unofficial app. It is not provided, approved, or endorsed by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government, the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation, or any other government agency or transport operator.**

### Main official information sources

- Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation: https://www.kotsu.metro.tokyo.jp/eng/
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government Bureau of Social Welfare: https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/
- Association for Open Data of Public Transportation: https://www.odpt.org/

Timetables, service information, fares, and program rules may change. For important journeys, please also check the latest official information.
